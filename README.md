## Featured Projects

### [Pennsylvania Democratic Voter Shift, 2020–2024](https://github.com/MutchkaJS/0101_Democratic_Voter_Shift_PA)
County-level analysis of why Democratic vote share moved between 2020 and 2024. Combines American Community Survey demographic indicators with county election results, then separates the shift into **turnout decline** versus **persuasion loss**.
`R` · `Python` · `Ridge regression` · `choropleth mapping` · `time-series forecasting`

### [Understanding Independent Voters](https://github.com/MutchkaJS/0701_PoliticalAffiliation_Understanding_Independent_Voters)
Models how demographic characteristics relate to party affiliation, and locates independent voters relative to Democratic and Republican voters — an audience-segmentation problem framed for campaign and advocacy use.
`classification` · `demographic analysis` · `segmentation` · `visualization`
